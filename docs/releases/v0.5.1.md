## Feature

- Add an Android app for music playback and comic reading, with favorites, reading progress, desktop-compatible WebDAV sync, and a signed APK for Android 9+ on arm64 devices.
- Configure your Komiic account in comic settings and sign in automatically, with local password storage, session refresh, and sign-out.

## Improvement

- Remove redundant media libraries from Windows and macOS installations.

## Fix

- Fix saving AI keys and cloud-sync credentials on macOS through the system Keychain.
