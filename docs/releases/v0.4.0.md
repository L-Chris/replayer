## Feature

- Sync AI configuration, comic favorites and reading progress, and online music favorites and playback progress across devices through WebDAV, with optional encrypted API key sync.
- Manage AI providers and model lists in Settings, select subtitle and comic translation models independently, and keep credentials in Windows Credential Manager.
- Delete individual playback history entries and undo accidental removals.

## Improvement

- Use simpler settings with aligned fields, switches, and a Save button that appears when changes are pending.
- Enjoy a refreshed app logo and consistent navigation icons throughout the player.
- Browse favorite comics on the home bookshelf and favorite media on the music and video pages.
- Read comics with compact floating controls, centered images, and favorite update checks on startup and when returning home.
- See an update prompt and release notes when a newer version is available.

## Fix

- Fix fullscreen leaving a black strip at the bottom and showing window controls during fullscreen playback or reading.
- Keep the music page's tabs accessible when lyrics are displayed.
- Prevent visible comic covers from flickering and repeatedly loading when the cover cache fills up.
